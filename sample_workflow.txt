AI Multi-Agent System - Sample Execution

Input Topic:
Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Workflow:
1. Coordinator receives the topic.
2. Research Agent gathers key information.
3. Coordinator passes the research to Writer Agent.
4. Writer Agent prepares a structured report.
5. Final report is displayed and saved as final_report.txt.

Expected Output:
A report containing:
- Title
- Introduction
- Key Findings
- Conclusion
